Privacy Policy

We appreciate your interest in our website. Protecting your personal data is very important to us. Below you will find essential information on how we process and protect your personal data.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

Kufsteiner Denkmal-, Fassaden- und Gebäudereinigung GmbH
Kaiserjägerstraße 26, 6330 Kufstein, Austria
E-mail: office@reinigung-kufstein.tirol
Phone: +43 676 3306650 / +43 676 4206012

2. Collection and Processing of Data

When you visit our website, basic technical information is automatically collected. This information (server log files) includes browser type, operating system, IP address, time of access, and pages visited. This data is technically necessary to ensure the stability, functionality, and security of the website. No personal profiling or analysis takes place.

Additionally, we only process personal data that you voluntarily provide, for example when contacting us via e-mail or our contact form (such as name, e-mail, phone number, date, and requested service). These data are used solely to process your enquiry.

3. Cookies

Our website uses cookies in order to provide a functional and user-friendly experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although this may limit some features of the website.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Necessary Cookies: Required for basic website functions, such as storing language preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: If active, these may help us improve our website by collecting anonymous usage data. You may opt out by disabling cookies in your browser.

4. Contact Form

If you contact us via our form or by e-mail, the information provided will be stored for the purpose of processing your request. We do not share this data with third parties unless legally required.

5. Data Sharing

Your data will only be shared if legally required (e.g., by authorities) or if you have given explicit consent. We do not transfer personal data to countries outside the EEA unless necessary for providing a specific service and only with appropriate data protection safeguards.

6. Security Measures

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, and alteration. Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption to ensure secure data transfer.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to request information about the data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to request deletion of your data, provided there is no legal requirement to retain it.
  • Right to restrict processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to object to the processing of your personal data.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at: office@reinigung-kufstein.tirol

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal or functional changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
