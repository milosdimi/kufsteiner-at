Ozone Cleaning

Ozone cleaning is a natural and highly effective method for disinfection and odour neutralisation – without any chemicals. Ozone destroys bacteria, viruses, mould spores and unpleasant odours in a chemical-free way.

Applications:

  • Apartments and holiday homes after guests
  • Vehicle interiors (smoke, pets)
  • Offices, surgeries and fitness studios
  • Basements, storage rooms and damp areas

Benefits:

  • No chemical residues – 100% natural
  • Effective against germs, mould and allergens
  • Long-lasting and thorough odour neutralisation
