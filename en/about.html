About us

Your full-service cleaning company from Kufstein

Welcome to Kufsteiner GmbH! As a family-owned company based in Kufstein, we have been delivering cleanliness that truly impresses since 2005 – from maintenance and window cleaning through to innovative dry ice blasting. What began with a small team dedicated to caring for historic buildings has grown into a reliable partner for private clients and businesses alike.

Our philosophy is built on tradition and innovation: we combine over 20 years of experience with modern techniques to offer tailor-made solutions. Sustainability is at the heart of what we do – we rely on eco-friendly cleaning methods and local resources to minimise our ecological footprint.
